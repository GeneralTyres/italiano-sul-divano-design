The project

Italiano sul divano started on my own sofa, with a laptop and a handful of students. Then I began travelling, and the work came with me. That turned out to be the whole idea: the lesson happens wherever you are comfortable.

On the sofa at home, on your friend's, on a bench in the park, on an empty beach. The place is yours, and so is the lesson. Nothing off a shelf: an hour built for you, your reasons for learning, and the pace that actually suits you.
